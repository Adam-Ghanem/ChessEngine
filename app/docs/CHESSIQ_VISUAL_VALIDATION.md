# ChessIQ Visual Validation

## Desktop, 1440px

The ChessIQ wordmark, violet/teal signal system, and Calculation Grid are immediately visible without displacing the board. The analysis corridor remains board-first: the board occupies the dominant central plane, the evaluation spine stays physically attached to it, and the rail turns the selected move into a legible sequence of engine reading, Analysis Thread, explanation, and review moments. The graph and timeline share the selected move state and remain subordinate to the position.

## Mobile, 390px

The mobile view keeps the board at maximum practical width, followed by thumb-reachable transport controls and compact notation. The rail follows with an engine-first explanation sequence and the graph is intentionally placed after analysis detail. No horizontal page overflow is visible. The Try Again feature is available from a critical moment and opens a bottom sheet for practice-specific secondary information.

## Observed quality checks

Keyboard-visible focus styles, labelled controls, text-bearing classification badges, reduced-motion behavior, dark/light themes, and local-sample data boundaries remain implemented. The visible data remains explicitly labelled as a local development sample; it is not presented as a user’s analysis history or personal statistics.

## Board reference refinement

The board now uses an ivory-and-moss tournament palette, higher-contrast coordinate labels, larger outlined white pieces, and heavier charcoal pieces with dimensional shadowing. This preserves ChessIQ’s violet selection and teal engine cues while making the play surface read more like a clean, familiar competitive chessboard. The reference influenced the board treatment only; ChessIQ branding, surrounding layout, and interaction language remain original.
