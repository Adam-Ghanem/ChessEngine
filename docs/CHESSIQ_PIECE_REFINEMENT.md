# ChessIQ Piece Refinement

ChessIQ now uses original scalable SVG pieces rather than font glyphs. The six silhouettes have been redrawn around the clarified visual target: **thick charcoal outline**, rounder proportions, broad bases, visually distinct crowns, a rounded bishop mitre, a larger horse-head knight, and high-contrast ivory versus charcoal materials. The reference informed the product language, but no third-party art assets or product branding were copied.

## Motion behavior

The existing source-to-destination movement system remains intact. It includes travel, a destination handoff that removes visible duplicate pieces, capture impact, castling-rook movement, promotion flare, selection lift, and reduced-motion support.

## Responsive validation

The bold silhouettes are clear at the desktop board scale and remain recognizable on the 390px mobile board without overflow or cramped board geometry.
