# ChessIQ Piece System Diagnosis and Correction

The earlier vector set was rejected because its crowns, bases, and interior strokes were over-detailed and inconsistent at board scale. The final correction replaces it with a simpler flat family built around the reference’s hierarchy: charcoal or ivory fill, one thick dark outline, broad rounded bases, large crowns, a single bishop slit, and a recognisable compact knight profile.

The pieces were checked at desktop and 390px mobile board sizes. Their colour contrast, silhouette separation, and board fit remain clear, while the existing travel, capture, castling, promotion, and reduced-motion behavior is preserved.
